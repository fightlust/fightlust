From Rhythm to Revelation: Building Daily Prayer and Scripture Habits for Spiritual Warfare

This episode breaks down the bookend rhythm of prayer—morning and evening practices that anchor your day in God’s presence—and the tactical power of Scripture as a weapon. You’ll learn how to imprint Scripture into your mind, use targeted verses to fight specific battles, and reframe worship as a weapon to shift spiritual atmospheres. Practical tools like the 3x5x7 system, hagah meditation, and tactical verse libraries will equip you to move from passive survival to active spiritual victory.
