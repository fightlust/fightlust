From Performance to Presence: Leading as a Son of the Kingdom

This episode explores how leaders can avoid the trap of performance-based identity and instead lead from their secure identity as sons of God. We’ll discuss the dangers of viewing leadership as a role rather than a relationship, the transformative joy of seeing others restored, and practical steps to protect your home and ministry. You’ll learn how to lead with covenant love, not relational consumption, and why your freedom is the foundation of your mission.
