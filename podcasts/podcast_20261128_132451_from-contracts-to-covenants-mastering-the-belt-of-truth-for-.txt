From Contracts to Covenants: Mastering the Belt of Truth for Lasting Freedom

This episode explores how to shift from transactional 'contract' thinking to sacred 'covenant' living, rooted in God’s design for sexuality and relationships. We’ll unpack the sacred fusion of the One-Flesh mystery, the layers of bonding in intimacy, and how covenants create lasting freedom through promise-based commitment. Practical tools include sealing relational 'gaskets,' reclaiming covenantal vision, and applying the 3x5x7 system to rewire your mind for covenantal living.
