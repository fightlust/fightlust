From Freedom to Commission: Becoming a Living Sanctuary in the Kingdom

This episode explores how your journey from personal restoration to global impact transforms your purpose, relationships, and legacy. You'll learn to radiate contagious freedom, avoid spiritual burnout, and anchor your life in the return of Christ—becoming sacred space where others encounter God's light.

================================================================

In the book *fightlust*, Book 5, *The Helmet of Salvation*, we turn to Chapter 19 — *The Expanding Horizon* — where I talk about how your freedom isn’t just about you. It’s not a private trophy to hide under your bed or a story to whisper in recovery meetings. It’s a seed for a global harvest. Jesus didn’t save you to make you comfortable; He saved you to make you dangerous to darkness. Think about that for a second. Your restored life — the way you walk, talk, and carry yourself — becomes a beacon for others trapped in the same cages you once were. It’s not about your marriage being perfect; it’s about showing a broken world what God’s ‘Very Good’ design looks like. When you shift from asking ‘How am *I* doing?’ to ‘How is the world doing?’ something shifts in your soul. You stop obsessing over your own little temptations and start seeing the war for souls around you. That’s where the real power comes in. This episode is about contagious freedom — living so boldly with joy, integrity, and hope that people can’t help but notice. It’s about becoming a walking sanctuary where others find rest, not just for yourself but for the world. We’ll unpack how to radiate this freedom in your neighborhood, your relationships, and your daily grind. I’ll also talk about the marathon of staying in love with the First Love — how to keep your spiritual fire burning for decades, not just days. And we’ll end with the weight of the King’s return — how living with ‘That Day’ in view changes everything. This isn’t just about surviving your past; it’s about commissioning your future to lead others out of darkness. Let’s dive in. Here's the thing about freedom—it doesn't work when you try to explain it. People don't need another lecture; they need to see it living in someone's skin. When you walk through life with quiet joy that doesn't depend on your mood, with integrity that doesn't bend for convenience, and with honor that treats everyone like they matter, that's when people start leaning in. Your face becomes a mirror of peace they can't explain. In your neighborhood, folks notice how you tell the truth even when it's awkward. In your Legion group, they feel the hope in your voice when you talk about the King's rescue. You don't have to force the conversation—you just keep showing up as the person who's already been set free. When someone asks, 'What's different about you?' you don't have to answer right away. Just invite them into your world for a while. Let them see how you carry freedom in your eyes, how your laughter doesn't hide shame, how your 'no' to temptation feels lighter than their 'yes.' Over time, they'll taste the peace you already know. That's how freedom multiplies—not by convincing people, but by becoming a living sanctuary they can walk into. You don't have to be loud or perfect. Just stay rooted in the truth that your freedom isn't yours to hoard—it's a gift meant to set others free. And when you lead someone out of the cage you once called home? That's when you really start to understand what it means to be a warrior for the Light. The fight for purity isn’t a 90-day sprint—it’s a fifty-year marathon. Here’s the thing: in twenty years, you’ll still be a new creation, still wearing the armor, still leading others out of cages. But the fire of the early days will fade if you’re not careful. That’s when spiritual professionalism creeps in—you turn your morning watch into a checklist instead of a quiet chat with the Commander, lead your team like an expert instead of a brother still in awe of grace, and start tracking stats instead of savoring the Cross. To stay fresh, you’ve got to wake up every day like it’s your first morning free. Let the miracle of clean eyes feel new. Let the whisper of the Father in the quiet remind you why you’re running this race. Your legacy isn’t about how many days you’ve ‘won’ or how strict your routine is. It’s about passing on a life where freedom isn’t a chore—it’s a gift you cherish, a story you live, and a light you hand to your kids. When you stay in love with the grace that saved you, the long haul stops feeling like a grind and starts feeling like a walk through the King’s garden. And that? That’s how you build a legacy your grandkids will still feel. Here's the thing about the return of Jesus—it's not just some far-off idea. It's the lens through which you live your life right now. When you fix your eyes on the day the trumpet sounds and the enemy gets thrown into the pit for good, the little temptations and distractions of this world start to look like nothing. I'm talking about the kind of focus that makes you realize the fleeting pleasures out there are just... well, swill. They don’t even compare to the high-voltage glory of eternity. You start acting like a citizen of the new heaven and new earth, not someone stuck in this broken world. Every time you choose to live with that end goal in mind, you’re putting on the final piece of your spiritual armor—the helmet that keeps you anchored. It’s not about ignoring life now; it’s about living in the power of what’s coming. You know Jesus is coming back to finish the work He started on the Cross. That’s the truth that changes everything. When you carry that weight, the things that once pulled you down—temptations, old habits, the noise of the world—they just don’t have the same grip anymore. You’re not waiting around for heaven; you’re living like it’s already yours, and that changes how you walk, talk, and lead. The future is so bright, the present doesn’t look tempting anymore. That’s the power of an apocalyptic focus. You’re not just surviving today; you’re ruling in the reality of His return. And when that day comes, and you see Him face to face, you’ll wear the crown He promised—not because you earned it, but because He won the victory. That’s the focus that keeps you steady when everything else feels shaky. Here’s the thing: when you become sacred space, your whole life starts to glow. Your body? It’s not just your body anymore—it’s a temple where the Holy Spirit lives. You walk with that awareness, so every choice you make honors that truth. Your mind? It’s not a jumbled mess of old habits and lies—it’s a sanctuary filled with Kingdom thoughts, where peace and purpose replace the noise. Your relationships? They’re not just casual connections—they’re a phalanx of honor, a team that stands strong together, built on trust and mutual respect. And your future? It’s not just about surviving another day—it’s a legacy of light, a path you’re paving for your kids, your grandkids, and everyone who crosses your path. You stop trying to 'manage' your freedom and let it take charge. Wherever you go—whether it’s the office, the gym, or the grocery store—you bring the fragrance of Christ with you. You don’t just exist in those spaces; you transform them. You’re not a 'recovering addict' anymore. You’re a commissioned warrior, radiating authority and showing broken men exactly what healing looks like. Your story becomes a signpost, pointing others to the Great Physician. You carry the weight of the world’s need, but you don’t let it crush you. You’re free, not because you’re perfect, but because you’ve stopped hiding and started living boldly. And every time you show up as your true self—clear-eyed, grounded, and full of grace—you make room for others to do the same. That’s what sacred space looks like. That’s what you’ve become.
