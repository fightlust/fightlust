From Survival to Sovereignty: The Warrior's Journey Beyond the Battle

This episode explores the shift from daily survival in recovery to lasting transformation through heart excavation, Kingdom joy, and reclaiming mental freedom. We'll uncover why 'just not sinning' isn't enough, how to replace destructive habits with divine satisfaction, and the power of adoration over addiction. Practical tools include mapping healthy dopamine sources, breaking the vacuum cycle, and retraining your brain for freedom.
