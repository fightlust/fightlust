From Defense to Dominion: The Warrior's Shield of Faith and the Power of Worship

This episode explores the transformation from passive spiritual defense to proactive warfare through the strategic use of Scripture, worship, and disciplined focus. By sharpening the Sword of the Spirit with the 3x5x7 system, shifting mental frequencies through worship, and dethroning self-centered idols, listeners will learn to reclaim their minds, rewire their neural pathways, and occupy their lives with the glory of God.
