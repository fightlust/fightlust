Breaking the Silence: Exposing the Enemy's Tactics and the Power of Community

This episode exposes the four core tactics the enemy uses to isolate and trap believers in spiritual battles, while shattering the illusion of shame by revealing startling statistics about pornography in the church. You'll learn practical strategies to break isolation, reframe your struggle as spiritual warfare, and leverage community for victory.
