From Contagious Freedom to Sacred Legacy: Living the Great Commission in Your Daily Life

This episode unpacks how to radiate spiritual freedom through everyday life, stay rooted in the 'first love' of Christ through decades of discipleship, and anchor your identity in the return of the King. We’ll explore practical ways to become a 'living sanctuary' of the Kingdom and establish a permanent Standard Operating Procedure for ruling your life in Christ.

================================================================

In my book fightlust, Book 5, The Helmet of Salvation, we turn to Chapter 19 — The Expanding Horizon — where I talk about what it really means to live out the Great Commission in your everyday life. You don’t need to stand on a soapbox or become a full-time preacher. Most of us spread freedom through what I call ‘contagious freedom’ — living with such quiet joy, integrity, and honor that people start noticing the light in your eyes, the trust in your actions, and the hope you carry. Your life becomes a sanctuary where others feel safe, and your freedom becomes a gift you give without even trying. But here’s the hard part: staying radiant over the long haul. You’ve got to keep the awe of your rescue alive every single day. And then there’s the return of the King — not just a spiritual idea, but a daily anchor. When you live with the reality of Jesus coming back to set everything right, the temporary hits of the world lose their power. You stop chasing digital mirages and start carrying the fragrance of Christ wherever you go. By the end of this journey, you’re not just ‘clean’ — you’re a walking embassy of the Kingdom, a commissioned warrior leading others out of cages you’ve already escaped. In this episode, we’re breaking down how to keep that fire burning for decades, how to avoid spiritual burnout, and how to let your life become a beacon of the freedom you’ve been given. Let’s get into it. Here's the thing about staying pure—it's not a 90-day fix. It's a 50-year marathon.
