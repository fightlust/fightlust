From Rules to Relationship: Reclaiming Your Masterpiece in Christ

This episode explores the shift from legalistic rule-keeping to a deep, relational intimacy with God. It addresses reclaiming your body as a temple, overcoming residual shame, and transforming sexuality into worship. Practical steps include daily declarations, consecrated habits, and embracing your identity as a restored masterpiece.
