From Distortion to Dominion: Mastering Cognitive Reframing in the Kingdom

In this episode, we uncover how the enemy uses cognitive distortions like mind-reading, emotional reasoning, and labeling to sabotage your recovery and identity. You'll learn to spot these 'scouts' in your mind, apply the 2 Corinthians 10:5 Protocol to arrest lies, and reframe your thoughts with Gospel truth. Discover the power of the 'Evidence Locker' to anchor your faith in tangible victories, and reclaim your imagination to see life through the lens of your new identity in Christ. This is about turning spiritual warfare into mental freedom.
