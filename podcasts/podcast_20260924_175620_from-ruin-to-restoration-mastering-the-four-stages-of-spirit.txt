From Ruin to Restoration: Mastering the Four Stages of Spiritual Renewal

This episode unpacks the four transformative stages of restoration—Realization, Repentance, Renovation, and Radiation—and how they lead from brokenness to purpose. You'll learn to shift from regret to repentance, navigate residual pain with grace, and turn your past mistakes into a river of hope for others. Practical steps for rebuilding trust and embracing the Gospel of the Second Chance are included.
