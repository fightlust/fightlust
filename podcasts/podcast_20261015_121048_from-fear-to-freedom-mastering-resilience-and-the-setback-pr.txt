From Fear to Freedom: Mastering Resilience and the Setback Protocol

In this episode, we explore how to move from fear-based living to covenant-based confidence, using setbacks as fuel for growth. You’ll learn the 'falling forward' mindset to turn stumbles into breakthroughs, how to cultivate spontaneous integrity through grace, and the power of community in group restoration. We’ll also break down the Setback Protocol—practical steps to rise quickly, fix triggers, and declare victory over shame. This is your roadmap to becoming a resilient warrior in the Kingdom.
