From Isolation to Intimacy: Building Four-Dimensional Relationships in Christ

This episode explores the four essential dimensions of intimacy—spiritual, emotional, intellectual, and physical—and how they create a foundation for lasting connection. We’ll uncover how to meet God-given hungers for connection, comfort, significance, and adventure through Kingdom principles instead of counterfeit solutions. By aligning your needs with divine design, you’ll discover how to build relationships that satisfy the soul and resist the lies of isolation.
