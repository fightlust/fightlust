From Avoidance to Adoration: Mastering the Shield of Faith for Enduring Freedom

This episode unpacks the 90-Day Intensive as a tool for rewiring your brain, the power of declarations to shift your mindset, and how to navigate the digital firestorm of ai-driven temptation. You'll learn how to move from mere avoidance to holy adoration of Jesus, build endurance through consistent discipline, and protect your mind in an era of hyper-personalized attacks. Practical steps include daily check-ins, yellow-flag awareness, and the rise protocol for setbacks.

================================================================

In my book Only Faith, Book 4, The Shield of Faith, we turn to Chapter 30 — the summary and action steps for Lesson 20, where we’ve been learning how to guard your heart through focus. If you’ve been following along, you know this isn’t just about avoiding sin anymore. It’s about moving from survival mode to living in the freedom God already gave you. We’ve talked about fixing your eyes on Jesus, trading a ‘not-falling’ mindset for a ‘constantly arising’ one, and even learning to see your setbacks as opportunities to strengthen your spiritual armor. But here’s the thing: freedom isn’t just a feeling. It’s a discipline. That’s why we’re diving into the 90-Day Intensive — a battle-proven strategy to rewire your brain, recalibrate your habits, and turn your ‘I can’t’ into ‘I won’t.’ Think of it like training for a marathon. You don’t show up on race day and expect to run 26 miles if you’ve been couchsurfing all year. Recovery works the same way. Your brain needs time — about three months — to reset its wiring, replace old patterns with new ones, and build a life where purity isn’t a struggle but a reflex. But this isn’t just about willpower. It’s about vision. You’re not gritting your teeth through 90 days of misery. You’re running toward something beautiful: a marriage you can be proud of, a legacy you’ll leave behind, and a relationship with God that’s deeper than anything the enemy could ever offer. And today, we’re breaking down exactly how to launch this intensive — from daily check-ins with your accountability partner to spotting those sneaky ‘yellow flags’ before they turn into full-blown crises. This isn’t a magic fix. It’s a war strategy. And if you’ve ever felt like you’re just waiting for the next stumble, this is your chance to become a warrior who holds the line, even when the wind howls and the enemy screams. Let’s get into it. Here’s the thing: speaking truth over your life isn’t just fancy words—it’s how you reprogram your mind to fight for your heart. When you declare things like ‘I’m moving from avoidance to adoration’ or ‘I evict every squatter; my imagination belongs to the King,’ you’re not just sounding strong. You’re training your brain to notice what’s true, not what the enemy whispers. Pair that with daily check-ins to your accountability partner—like a quick text saying ‘Day 22. Shield’s up. Still fighting’—and you create a habit of staying connected when the world tries to isolate you. Then there’s the ‘yellow-flag’ thing: every evening, you scan for little shifts—did you skip prayer? Did stress creep in? Did you tell yourself ‘I’ve got this’ and let your guard down? Catching those flags early means a simple correction, like calling a brother in the Legion or diving into the Word, keeps you from hitting a red-flag crisis. And if you do stumble? The rise protocol helps you find the exact crack in your wall—was it your phone in the bedroom? A grudge you’re holding?—and patch it with a specific fix, like charging your phone in the kitchen or praying for forgiveness. The key is, you don’t reset to Day 1. You keep moving toward Day 90, because that 90-day season isn’t about perfection. It’s about rewiring your brain, letting the fog lift, and building a new normal where purity feels natural. And through it all, you’re not just avoiding sin—you’re chasing the presence of Jesus, filling your heart with the One who already set you free. That’s how you stay strong in the ‘long middle,’ when the sky looks clear but the real battle is for your imagination and your joy. You keep your shield raised because you know the war isn’t over, but you’re not a victim anymore. You’re a warrior with a mission, and every day is a step toward living the freedom you already have. Avoidance alone isn’t enough. If you just focus on staying away from sin, you’re leaving your heart empty—and that’s a problem. The enemy knows empty spaces get filled, so he’ll send whatever he can to take over. But when you fill your soul with adoration for Jesus, everything changes. It’s not just about avoiding bad stuff anymore; it’s about chasing intimacy with the King. When your heart is captivated by Him, the battle becomes an act of worship. You’re not trying to be good—you’re living in the presence of the One who makes you good. The fleeting thrill of sin? It’s nothing compared to the joy of knowing God. Purity stops being a performance and starts being a byproduct of being with Him. You’re not just saying no to lust; you’re saying yes to a relationship that satisfies your soul deeper than anything else ever could. It’s like trading a quick fix for a forever fix. When you fix your eyes on Jesus, the war isn’t about white-knuckling your way through—it’s about letting His love flood your heart so there’s no room for anything else. You’re not just fighting temptation; you’re falling in love with the One who set you free. And that love? It’s stronger than any fleeting pleasure. It’s the kind of love that turns battles into blessings and makes the hard stuff worth it, because you’re not just surviving—you’re thriving in His presence. Here’s the deal: artificial intelligence has turned the digital battlefield into a high-stakes game where temptation is faster, smarter, and way more personal. The enemy isn’t just launching random arrows at your shield anymore—he’s using ai to study your habits, your triggers, and your weaknesses, then firing hyper-targeted content right at you. Think about it: algorithms now know when you’re tired, stressed, or lonely, and they use that to push exactly what you’re most vulnerable to. Filters alone aren’t enough anymore, and accountability isn’t optional—it’s non-negotiable. Your phone isn’t just a tool; it’s a weapon the enemy wants to hijack. You have to treat it like a loaded gun: lock it down, guard it fiercely, and use it to fuel your fight for freedom instead of feeding your flesh. Ai makes the attacks feel inevitable, but your shield of faith is still your best defense. The key? Stay hyper-aware, double down on accountability partners, and never let your guard drop in the long middle when the sky looks clear. This isn’t just about avoiding bad content—it’s about reclaiming your mind, your attention, and your purpose. The digital firestorm is real, but so is your power to stand firm when you’re armed with truth, community, and a plan to protect your heart. Hey, so when you mess up, it’s not the end of the world—it’s a chance to get smarter about your weaknesses. Think of it like this: every time you stumble, the enemy thinks he’s won, but really, you’re just gathering intel. You don’t wallow in shame or let pride shut you down. Instead, you ask, ‘Where did I break? Was it a specific time, place, or emotion? Did I let my guard down when I was tired, stressed, or alone?’ Once you spot the hole, you don’t just patch it with vague promises. You get specific. If it was your phone in the bedroom, you move it to the kitchen and buy an alarm clock. If it was unprocessed anger, you write out a forgiveness prayer and say it daily. The key is action—simple, doable steps that turn your ‘oops’ into a stronger wall. And here’s the thing: if you slip up on Day 45, you don’t throw away the first 45 days of work. You pick up where you left off, fix the breach, and keep going. The 90-Day Intensive isn’t about being perfect—it’s about progress. Every patch you add, every boundary you build, makes your fortress harder to crack. It’s like training for a marathon: one misstep doesn’t mean you quit. You adjust your stride, refocus, and keep running. Over time, those little fixes stack up. What starts as a shaky shield becomes a solid wall. You’re not just surviving—you’re learning to thrive in the long middle, where the real battle is won or lost. So next time you trip, don’t panic. Breathe, ask questions, take action, and remember: your fortress is built one brick at a time, and every setback is just another brick waiting to be laid.
