This episode explores the sacred shift from transactional relationships to covenantal commitments, revealing how to protect your intimacy, reject self-serving contracts, and embrace God’s design for lasting unity. Learn practical steps to seal your marriage, retrain your mind, and build a life rooted in promise, not performance.

Here's the thing about relationships—whether it's with God, your spouse, or even yourself—they run on one of two models: contracts or covenants. A contract is like a temporary deal where everyone’s keeping score. You do your part, but if the other person messes up or you find a better option, you bail. It’s all about what you can get. That’s how the world works, right? You sign up for something, and if it stops paying off, you move on. But covenants? They’re totally different. A covenant is a lifelong promise where you commit no matter what—good times or bad, even if it costs you. It’s not about what you can get, it’s about what you can give. Think about how ancient covenants worked: people would cut animals, walk through the pieces, and say, ‘If I break this promise, let this happen to me.’ It was serious business. God showed Abraham the ultimate covenant by walking through the sacrifice alone, taking the penalty Himself. That’s the kind of unconditional love we’re talking about. Your relationship with God isn’t a contract where you earn His favor—it’s a covenant He already sealed with His life.
