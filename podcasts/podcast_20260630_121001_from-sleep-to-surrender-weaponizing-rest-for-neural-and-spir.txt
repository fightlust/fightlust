From Sleep to Surrender: Weaponizing Rest for Neural and Spiritual Victory

In this episode, we explore how sleep and rest are not just biological necessities but high-stakes spiritual strategies. By integrating neuroscience with biblical truth, we uncover how sleep cleanses the brain, protects willpower, and becomes a nightly act of surrender to God. Practical protocols for a 'Zero-Tech' Sabbath, the glymphatic system's role in neural health, and repelling the inner drive to strive will equip you to fight the war for purity with a rested mind and a restored soul.
