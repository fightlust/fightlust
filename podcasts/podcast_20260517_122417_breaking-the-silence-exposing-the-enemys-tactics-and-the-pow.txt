Breaking the Silence: Exposing the Enemy's Tactics and the Power of Community

This episode uncovers the enemy's four core strategies—Isolation, Deception, Accusation, and Repetition—and reveals how these tactics trap believers in cycles of shame and addiction. We’ll break down the C.A.S.T. Protocol (Connect, Announce Truth, Silence the Accuser, Take the New Path) as a practical tool for immediate spiritual counter-assault. Then, we’ll confront the shocking reality of a silent epidemic in the church, where 68% of Christian men and 33% of women struggle with pornography. By reframing the struggle as a common spiritual battle and not a personal failure, we’ll explore how transparency, community, and Scripture dismantle shame and ignite collective victory.
