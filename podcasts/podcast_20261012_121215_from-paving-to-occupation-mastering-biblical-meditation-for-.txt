From Paving to Occupation: Mastering Biblical Meditation for Automatic Victory

Discover how to transform Scripture from abstract truth into personal power through biblical meditation. Learn to personalize verses, visualize victory, and shift from manual effort to spontaneous freedom. This episode unpacks the neuroscience of neural reprogramming, handling 'addiction ghosts,' and finding ultimate satisfaction in God’s presence—so you can move from struggling to simply living as a conqueror.
