From Distortion to Dominion: Mastering Cognitive Reframing for Spiritual Clarity

This episode equips listeners to identify and dismantle seven cognitive distortions that sabotage recovery and spiritual clarity. By learning to 'spot the scouts' of the enemy—like all-or-nothing thinking, overgeneralization, and emotional reasoning—we replace lies with Kingdom truth. Through practical reframing techniques rooted in 2 Corinthians 10:5, we transform our mindset from reactive to reclaimed, turning doubt into divine perspective.
